## 1. Test Objectives

1. Prove that cross-device continuity executes reliably across two physically distinct machines.
2. Verify that network endpoints (`flux_peer_id`, `zarya_url`, `flux_url`) propagate without localhost fallback or leak.
3. Validate deterministic SHA-256 payload integrity end-to-end.
4. Verify that the S16 continuity pipeline halts cleanly across all failure modes without false positives.
5. Ensure 100% regression freedom across the complete test suite.
